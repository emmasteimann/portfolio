SlimeLab

A physically simulated slime character that splats when it lands and pulls itself back into a dome: MLS-MPM on the GPU, a ray-marched surface shaded as a lens, bubbles, glow eyes and a coloured shadow. Built twice, in Unreal and Unity, and measured side by side.

  • 34.6k particles
  • 3.6–4.0 ms sim
  • −27–33% after optimizing
  • M5 Pro · Metal · 1440p

About

I'm a rendering engineer who likes the whole stack: GPU architecture and frame budgets at one end, and making something that looks and feels good at the other. I've shaped rendering at engine scale at Epic, built AR rendering at Niantic, and spent years before that freelancing on game engines, AR games and interactive murals.

I also mentor. Much of my best work has been tooling that lets technical artists move faster.
